## 2. Link to training

Commits `bfd2ab8` and `5a6b299`, in `src/nnslr_tools/brain_targets.py`, `brain_net.py`, `brain_train.py`. Full description: [NEURAL_NETWORK.md](../NEURAL_NETWORK.md), "The brain".

Ready:

- Each frame gets its own answers and masks, and what stays out of the loss is said frame by frame.
- One network: encoder, GRU, `speed_head` (the only output on the car) and `works_head` (training only). The loss is `speed + 0.3 * works`, and the works error reaches the GRU and the encoder that the speed uses too.
- The frozen split as a mask on the loss. A works site with a frame in test or validation has none in train, and a frame out of train never moves a weight.
- Whole drives in order, the state carried along, only the past: approach, crossing and exit are one sequence.
- Inputs by default: picture, signs read, car ahead, country. Gas, brake and own speed only with `--driver`, as a comparison.
- Measures on excluded places: speed, frames with no gas, frames 20 km/h or more from the car's own speed, inside the marks, works precision and recall, and per site how long after his end tap the speed follows.
- Dry run on his real labels: 2 steps on the CPU, 12 frames, the chain works, the label database unchanged. Nothing was learned.
